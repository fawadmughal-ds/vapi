SECTION 01

Overview & How Billing Works

To have a NextCall AI voice agent answer real phone calls, your number must reach our calling engine over the internet (SIP). There are three routes to achieve this. This document explains how each works, exactly where you need to go (Jazz / mobile operator or a local carrier), and the full cost breakdown so you can choose confidently.

Every route has two cost layers. The telephony layer changes per route; the NextCall portal layer is the same for all three.

Layer 1 — Telephony

The cost of the line / number itself: the SIM gateway device, the Twilio cloud number + forwarding, or the local SIP trunk & DID. This is paid to the hardware vendor or the telecom operator.

Layer 2 — NextCall Portal

The AI agent usage, billed by NextCall in credits (credits = talk minutes). Includes the dashboard, agents, analytics, call recordings and support.

Important — Pakistani numbers & Twilio Twilio does not sell voice-enabled Pakistani (+92) numbers. Route 2 therefore uses a foreign cloud number (US/UK) that your Jazz number forwards to. Routes 1 and 3 keep or provide a genuine local number. This is a regulatory limitation, not a NextCall limitation.

SECTION 02 — ROUTE A

The Three Routes to Go Live

1

SIM Gateway Device

Keep your exact Jazz / Zong number · one-time hardware
How it works
A small GSM/VoLTE gateway holds the physical SIM and converts it into a SIP line that connects to the NextCall AI agent. Callers dial the same number; the AI answers.
Best for
Clients who must keep their current Jazz number and expect steady call volume.
Step-by-step — where to go
  • Choose & buy the gateway deviceOrder a 1‑SIM GSM/VoLTE gateway (Yeastar / Dinstar) from a local VoIP hardware vendor. NextCall shares recommended models & a verified vendor.
  • Prepare the SIM (Jazz Business Center / franchise)Use the existing Jazz SIM, or get a Jazz Business SIM from a Jazz Business Center. Confirm a voice/minutes bundle suitable for your call volume.
  • Install the device on-siteInsert the SIM, connect the gateway to power and internet (LAN). A static IP or router port-forwarding is recommended.
  • NextCall connects the gateway to your agentWe configure the SIP trunk credential in the platform (BYO SIP) and link it to your AI agent — no number change.
  • Test & go liveWe run test calls, tune the agent, then hand over. Callers now reach the AI on your existing number.
Where you physically go Hardware vendor (device) → Jazz Business Center / franchise (SIM & bundle). No number porting needed.

2

Cloud Number + Call Forwarding (Twilio)

Fastest to launch · foreign number · monthly rental
How it works
NextCall provisions a Twilio cloud number connected to your AI agent. Your Jazz number is set to forward incoming calls to that number, so the AI answers every call.
Best for
Quick pilots, or businesses whose callers are mostly international.
Step-by-step — where to go
  • NextCall buys & configures the cloud numberWe provision a Twilio number (US/UK) and attach it to your AI agent. Nothing for you to install.
  • Enable international forwarding on your Jazz numberVisit a Jazz Business Center or call the Jazz helpline (111) and request that International Call Forwarding / IDD be enabled on your number (usually requires a postpaid/Business line).
  • Set the call-forward to our numberActivate “forward all calls” to the Twilio number NextCall provides. Typical GSM code: **21*<number># then dial; deactivate with ##21#. Jazz Business can also set this for you.
  • Test & go liveWe place test calls to your Jazz number to confirm the AI answers via the forwarded leg.
Cost warning — international forwarding Because the Twilio number is foreign, the Jazz forwarding leg is billed at Jazz’s international/IDD rate. For Pakistan-heavy call traffic this is usually the most expensive route per minute. Ideal for pilots, not high local volume.
Where you physically go Jazz Business Center / Jazz Helpline 111 (to enable + set international forwarding). NextCall handles Twilio.

3

New Local SIP Number (Nayatel / PTCL) + NextCall

Cleanest, legal, best for volume · genuine local +92 number
How it works
A licensed local carrier (Nayatel, PTCL, etc.) delivers a new local number (DID) over a SIP trunk that connects straight to the NextCall AI agent. The most reliable, fully-compliant setup.
Best for
Professional / long-term deployments and higher call volumes.
Step-by-step — where to go
  • Contact the carrier’s business salesReach Nayatel (nayatel.com/business/sip-trunk) or PTCL Enterprise (helpline 1218, care@ptcl.net.pk) and request a SIP Trunk + DID.
  • Order channels & DID number(s)Pick the number of concurrent channels (calls at once) and how many local numbers (DIDs) you need. They quote a monthly rate.
  • Carrier provisions the trunkThey deliver SIP credentials (host, username, password / IP) once your business documents & internet are in place.
  • NextCall connects the trunk to your agentWe add the SIP credential (BYO SIP) in the platform and bind your new local number to the AI agent.
  • Test & go liveWe validate quality & concurrency, then publish your new professional local number.
Where you physically go Nayatel / PTCL business sales (order SIP trunk + DID). NextCall does the technical connection.
